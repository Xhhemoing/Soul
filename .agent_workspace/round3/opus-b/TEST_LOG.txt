TWO FINDINGS THE NEW TRIPWIRES PRODUCED
=======================================
Both were written as assertions of what I believed, both failed on first run,
and both are recorded as they actually are rather than adjusted away.

FINDING 1 — the two A0 write modes are not indistinguishable on v0.1 data.
  I asserted that LastWriteWins and NoDowngrade agree on every v0.1-reachable
  log. They agree on position, band and lock; they disagree on evidence_ids.
  Five re-fills of one questionnaire:
      LastWriteWins  evidence_ids = [5]
      NoDowngrade    evidence_ids = [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
  The profile reads the same either way, so this is not a reason to unfreeze,
  but 「用户能复核计数」 is a criterion in its own right and a user asking "what
  is this based on" gets a shorter answer under the frozen mode.
  Now split into two tests that say exactly this:
      the_frozen_write_mode_decides_nothing_a_v01_user_is_told        PASS
      the_two_write_modes_do_differ_in_what_they_cite                 PASS
  src/a0.rs module docs corrected to match. Round X question, flagged in
  REPORT.md §6.

FINDING 2 — "only a correction reaches Strong" is a property of the v0.1 data
  plane, not of A0's code.
  A0 reads EvidenceRef::band and never caps it. A producer that writes a row
  labelled Strong gets Strong with no correction involved; under NoDowngrade a
  later agreeing questionnaire answer does not pull it back, because the band
  of a supported position is the strongest single supporter. Unreachable in
  v0.1 — record_axis_inference has no production caller — which is exactly why
  the guarantee survives today.
  Recorded as two tests, in the shape last_write_wins_is_the_a0_contract uses:
      strong_on_the_retained_path_means_the_user_said_so   PASS  (scoped to v0.1)
      a0_trusts_the_band_on_a_row_it_is_handed             PASS  (the gap, pinned)
  NOT fixed, because A0 is frozen and the fix is a behaviour change. REPORT.md
  §6 hands it to whichever version starts writing behavioural rows.
